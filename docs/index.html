The apps

Transmitter

Mac

The master.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon or Intel
Download v2.13.8

Transmitter Light

Mac

A simpler Transmitter for stereo work. Sends 2 channels of audio plus picture from a screen or window capture, a Blackmagic input or a camera, with the same low delay and encryption.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon or Intel
Download v2.13.8

Transmitter Viewer

Mac

A simple Transmitter player that plays back live video and audio from the Transmitter sender. Plays back in sync with low delay, always encrypted. Runs on any Mac on the same network or across the internet. Also plays back local video files, locked to the sender's timecode using the sender's audio.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon or Intel
Download v2.13.8

Transmitter Receiver

Mac

A dedicated receiver app for Mac meant for professional studios to stream video with multi-channel audio (2.0 to Atmos 7.1.4) in sync — either as a desktop window or using a Blackmagic output card. Works with Pro Tools or any DAW. Can also play back local picture files of most formats, including Avid formats, in sync using remote MTC timecode from the main Transmitter app, locking to Pro Tools.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon or Intel
Download v2.13.8

Transmitter Apple TV

Apple TV

Watch Transmitter streams on your Apple TV. Plays back live video and audio from the Transmitter sender in sync, with low delay, always encrypted.

Requires tvOS · Apple TV HD or Apple TV 4K
